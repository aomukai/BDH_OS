# Ninereeds curriculum v6 — synthesis decisions

## Outcome

V6 schedules **396 acquisition lessons** and **270 rehearsal/transfer lessons**, for **666 precommitted conducted lessons**. Conditional diagnostics and remediation add nothing unless evidence triggers them. The acquisition count is deliberately above the approximate 300 expectation: after counting actual constructions, response forms, lexical activations, discourse operations, and world distinctions, 300 could be met only by recreating v4-style bundles or v5-style deferral. The total program remains inside the requested 600–700 envelope.

Lesson 000 is the preserved source lesson and the sole staged exception. The first explicit ordinary mind/person/animal/device ontology is **L177**, after 176 post-bootstrap ordinary-language acquisitions. Early Errol appearances remain implicit phone-mediated dialogue. The intentional primitive “Errol travels by data transfer” is taught later as its own world distinction and is contrasted separately with message transfer and physical travel.

## What v4 and v5 revealed

The reconstructed Deep Research v4 is a coverage-maximizing compiler. It has 295 sequence entries backed by 157 world objectives, 131 point records, 288 lexeme bundles, and 12 entries labeled as transfer. It gives every C001–C240 candidate an active compiled mapping. Its strongest contributions are explicit provenance, broad K–8 world coverage, persistent identifiers, and an attempt to expose prerequisites and surface-language contracts.

Those counts conceal load. A named lexeme bundle is often treated as one novelty even when it introduces several unrelated words or constructions; comparison, passive, source, and discourse packages are especially dense. Some dependencies point to registry identifiers instead of demonstrated earlier surface language. V4 opens explicit Errol ontology almost immediately. It also mixes 12 transfer entries into the acquisition sequence. Its visual decision is postponed: 233 lessons are `PB-O`, 61 are direct-sequence (`DS`), one is `PB-N`, and none is unambiguously required. V6 accepts v4's breadth and provenance discipline, but rejects those grouping, ordering, separation, and asset assumptions.

DeepSeek v5 is a closure-first checkpoint. It compiles 63 lessons, marks 72 candidates active and 10 consolidated, and defers 158. Its best decisions are the explicit principal-novelty field, counted frontier language, attention to required established language, Lesson 000 as the sole staged exception, later explicit ontology, and Bob's provisional status.

V5's restraint becomes undercoverage. It stops before most science, geography, civics, economics, computing, literature, culture, and formal academic language. Several surviving lessons still bundle multiple constructions or identity operations. One transfer lesson remains inside acquisition. Its visual plan contains 63 regular-image requirements and 690 images by validator count, but zero picture books; the curriculum marks 54 lessons `no` and nine `optional`, while the asset plan lists three optional book candidates. Generic stand-ins also discard opportunities to build the canonical village. V6 accepts v5's closure audit and explicit counts, but rejects stopping at 63, mass deferral, transfer leakage, and the no-required-book assumption.

The disagreement is informative rather than something to average. V4 demonstrates the coverage cost of K–8 preparation; v5 demonstrates the prerequisite and semantic-cohesion cost of teaching that coverage honestly. V6 pays both costs: it restores the world model, splits hidden novelty, and schedules transfer separately.

## Authority resolutions

- The identity policy controls all self-identity and integrity content. Ninereeds is Ninereeds, is a mind, learns, and has bounded knowledge. No lesson classifies Ninereeds through implementation, substrate, consciousness, sentience, AI, LLM, model, or machine relations.
- The world bible controls characters, locations, topology, chronology, and Errol. Errol is a mind communicating through Gran's phone; Errol is not the phone, screen, case, symbol, or a body.
- Actual Lesson 000 is referenced rather than re-authored. Its controlled drills are noncanonical and it has no picture book.
- No explicit operator approval for Bob was found in the reviewed authoritative sources. Bob therefore appears only in L000, remains provisional, and has no invented biography.
- Candidate C001–C240 records are provenance, not commands. The source accounting has 240 records: 206 active and 34 consolidated, with no silent omissions. A consolidated disposition means the candidate's actual facets were decomposed or shared; it does not mean its original bundle was copied.
- Visual grounding is a prerequisite substrate. The 2,500-entry preparation lexicon and reviewed variation do not establish syntax, discourse competence, fluency, chronology, or a complete world model.

## Phase architecture

- `P0_BOOTSTRAP`: 1 — Preserved bootstrap
- `P1_REFERENCE`: 45 — Concrete reference and the first persistent places
- `P2_ACTION_SOCIAL`: 60 — Action, routine, cooperation, and communication repair
- `P3_OPERATORS`: 71 — Quantity, time, comparison, causation, material, and travel operators
- `P4_ONTOLOGY_EPISTEMIC`: 49 — Explicit Errol ontology, communication technology, identity, and epistemic integrity
- `P5_NATURAL_QUANTITATIVE_WORLD`: 80 — Natural and quantitative world spiral
- `P6_SOCIAL_TECHNICAL_WORLD`: 58 — Social, historical, cultural, economic, literary, and computing world spiral
- `P7_FORMAL_BRIDGE`: 32 — Formal language and academic reasoning bridge

P1–P3 deliberately establish reference, action, repair, quantity, time, comparison, cause, material, and travel before explicit ontology. P4 then separates Errol, phone, symbol, body, message, stored data, transfer, source, evidence, record, authorship, and current belief. P5 and P6 use those operators to build natural and social systems. P7 adds formal academic language only after its semantic relations are available in concrete domains.

## Granularity and surface closure

Each acquisition lesson declares one principal axis: language form, construction, response form, discourse operation, world distinction, or the authorized L000 staged exception. `actual_counts` counts frontier entries and construction/response/discourse units rather than trusting a bundle name. Contrastive alternatives may coexist in one lesson only when they form one teachable system, such as in/on or some/any, and the entries are still counted separately.

Four frontier entries is the default ceiling. The only planned post-L000 five-entry exceptions are 0–4, five perception verbs, and five sensory nouns; each has an explicit compiler note requiring separate evaluation and a split if learner evidence is weak. A lesson compiler may reduce a frontier, but may not add one to make an activity convenient.

`required_established_language` is an authoring closure contract. It is not a mastery declaration: the local compiler must check actual learner evidence before freezing the next lesson. Foundation image dependencies may supply reviewed referents and variation, but they cannot be used as evidence that a sentence frame or discourse operation is known.

## Chronology and picture books

Canonical events advance through early autumn, mid/late autumn, early winter, late winter/early spring, Bello's mid-spring arrival, and late spring observations. Every lesson declares whether it creates a canonical event, reuses canonical state, or is noncanonical instruction. Bello's arrival is his sole canonical first encounter with Biscuit. No rehearsal creates another event or first meeting.

V6 makes the picture-book decision at plan time: 34 acquisition lessons require one, 362 do not, and none is left optional. Required books carry event order, object persistence, canonical topology, social interaction, organism continuity, or observation across time. “No” does not mean “no images”; it means ordinary reviewed variants, diagrams, canonical references, or dialogue are sufficient.

## Rehearsal and transfer

The 270 scheduled rehearsal entries introduce no language or facts. Two coverage passes retrieve every acquisition at least twice. Forty curated recombination families repeat useful POINTs under different compatible TOPICs; they are authored mappings, not random cross-products. Each entry records its spacing gap, interference set, grounding mode, response expectation, and evaluation objective. Production and world-model correctness are scored separately, and a reduced-scaffold retry follows correction.

Six conditional gates diagnose reference, action/social language, operators, identity/epistemics, world systems, and academic transfer. A gate opens only after repeated evidence or a single high-impact identity/chronology/safety error. Remediation walks the smallest failed prerequisite chain and cannot add frontier material.

## Newly introduced decisions

- A hard separation between acquisition IDs (`L...`) and rehearsal IDs (`R...`).
- A twice-covering scheduled rehearsal stream plus curated semantic recombination.
- Required picture books for canonical and temporal grounding instead of blanket optionality.
- Explicit separation among retrieval, delivery, understanding, truth, source, evidence, storage, message travel, Errol's data-transfer travel, and physical travel.
- Explicit record/authorship/view-revision lessons under the identity policy rather than a monolithic “identity integrity” bundle.
- Hazard instructions are source- and scope-sensitive; no generic response is taught as universal.
- Ordinary “model” lessons concern simplified representations and contain an explicit ban on using that relation to classify Ninereeds.

Most importantly, the actual learner state at compilation time, operator approval for Bob, exact accepted assets, and future first learner-facing appearances of unused extended cast members are not inferred here.
