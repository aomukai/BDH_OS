## Reading the skeleton

`required_established_language` is a closed authoring vocabulary requirement, not a claim of learner mastery. The compiler must check actual learner evidence before freezing a lesson. `foundation_grounding_dependencies` identifies reviewed visual concepts that may support grounding; it never upgrades them to fluent language. `intended_later_rehearsal_targets` points to the concrete no-novelty schedule.
