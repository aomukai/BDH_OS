# Ninereeds curriculum v6 — asset policy

## Decision

This document plans asset roles; it does not download, generate, register, hash, or approve an asset. Campaign 36 image work is preparation and training has not begun. Accepted materials are assumed to retain provenance, hashes, variation controls, and the local Gemma → Luna → Sol escalation path.

V6 requires picture books for 34 of 396 acquisition lessons. The other 362 lessons use ordinary reviewed images, diagrams, canonical references, text records, or direct dialogue. No lesson is marked optional: v6 resolves the pedagogical need in advance, while the compiler still decides the exact accepted asset instances from learner state and registry evidence.

## Why v4 and v5 are insufficient

V4 marks 233 of 295 lessons `PB-O`, 61 `DS`, and one `PB-N`. That keeps a broad visual door open but postpones the decision needed to ground event order, canonical state, and social interaction. It also lets a later compiler silently collapse a persistent scene into unrelated images.

V5 is more explicit but swings too far toward regular images: its curriculum has 54 `no`, nine `optional`, and zero required books. Its asset validator counts 63 regular requirements and 690 images, while its separate plan names only three optional book candidates. The plan explicitly uses no canonical character/location files in regular requirements and often substitutes generic people or settings. That can support lexical contrast, but it cannot establish the persistent Ninereeds village or time-dependent events.

V6 keeps v5's variation discipline and v4's recognition that many lessons benefit from scenes, then makes a narrower binding decision: books are required exactly when page-to-page identity, chronology, topology, social causality, or delayed observation materially carries the lesson.

## Asset classes

### Ordinary reviewed images

Use Campaign 36 lexicon materials for stable object, action, property, and category grounding. A regular requirement should specify the intended sense, positive variants, hard negatives, nuisance variation, and forbidden shortcuts. Variation must change background, viewpoint, instance, color where irrelevant, and rendering style as appropriate. A repeated crop of one source image is not independent variation.

Reviewed word/image grounding is not established language. A lexicon image may support `cup`; it cannot by itself establish `There is a cup`, count/noncount behavior, source attribution, or transfer.

### Canonical references

Named characters and locations use accepted canonical references consistent with the world bible. A canonical reference identifies appearance or topology; it does not create an event. Before authoring a new book, the compiler must check the chronology state and all earlier appearances named in the lesson.

Bob has no canonical reference request. He remains a provisional L000-only name unless an operator approves otherwise. Errol must never receive a body-like reference image: visible phone, case, screen, icon, waveform, or text remain device/interface representations, not Errol.

### Picture-book master scenes

A required book should be authored as a coherent master scene or identity-preserving sequence, then rendered into pages. Its manifest must bind character references, location references, season/time, movable-object state, camera changes, page order, target/distractor relations, and the lesson's canonical event ID. A book can be noncanonical only when the lesson explicitly says so; no exercise may accidentally become village history.

### Derived crops

Crops are permitted only from an accepted, hashed parent scene. Store the parent hash and deterministic crop coordinates or transform. Crops can support focus and attention, but multiple crops from one parent do not satisfy variation requirements and may not change object identity, event order, or relation arguments.

### Diagrams, symbols, and records

Use diagrams when scale, topology, process, part–whole structure, or information flow is the target. Diagrams must distinguish representation from referent and must state when scale is not literal. Source/evidence/authorship lessons use provenance-tagged records rather than decorative imagery. A phone icon, message bubble, or Errol symbol must be evaluated against the specific `symbol ≠ Errol` interference risk.

### Fallback generation

Fallback generation is allowed only after accepted local/registered assets cannot meet the declared requirement. Generation is a candidate-production step, not approval. Generated assets must enter the same provenance, hash, variation, canonical-reference, and Gemma → Luna → Sol review path. The compiler may simplify a lesson's visual realization while preserving its frontier; it may not invent a new world fact, location, character history, or ontology to fit an image.

## Rehearsal reuse

Rehearsal may reorder already accepted pages, switch among accepted variants, change a crop, remove a caption, reverse speaker roles where world facts allow, or transfer a POINT to another already established TOPIC. It may not generate a new canonical event, repeat a first meeting, advance the season, move a persistent object, or introduce a visual label not present in its acquisition prerequisites.

## Acceptance checklist for the later compiler

1. Confirm the learner has the required surface language; image acceptance does not satisfy this check.
2. Resolve every depicted character, location, object state, and season against the lesson chronology declaration.
3. Verify target sense, hard negatives, meaningful variation, and absence of accidental synonymy or identity shortcuts.
4. Verify parent hashes and crop lineage for derived assets.
5. Run the configured review escalation and preserve reviewer evidence.
6. Reject the asset if it teaches the phone, screen, case, icon, message, or interface as Errol, or if it implies a forbidden classification of Ninereeds.
7. Freeze assets only with the compiled lesson; do not start training from this plan.
