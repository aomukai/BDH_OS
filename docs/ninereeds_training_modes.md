# Ninereeds training-purpose contracts

Every new campaign declares its purpose before any lesson, training job, or evaluation can be admitted. The declaration is immutable evidence, is hashed, and is bound into training-session plans and evaluation jobs. An evaluator may not silently replace the campaign's question with “did the score improve?”

Every run is still followed by both behavioral chat and MRI/activation analysis. Loss remains telemetry only. It cannot rank, admit, reject, continue, promote, or roll back a checkpoint in any mode.

## Modes

- **Bootstrap** develops a missing interface or foundational behavior from little or no prior capability. Its criteria are developmental milestones. Word production or sentence-like sequencing can therefore be meaningful progress even while semantic output remains nonsensical.
- **Advancement** teaches a new capability along one lineage. This is the only mode whose default comparison is candidate against parent and whose automatic guards may recommend admission or rollback.
- **Experimental** tests a declared hypothesis about Ninereeds, training, regression, recovery, or evaluation. Improvement is not required. Regression and surprising behavior are retained as evidence unless a predeclared failure criterion says the experiment itself was invalid.
- **Evolutionary** applies different declared training approaches to comparable branches. Each branch is preserved. No branch may be ranked and no winner may be proposed until every declared branch has complete chat and MRI evidence.
- **Merge** develops distinct specialist lineages and combines them through an explicit architecture-specific procedure. Source checkpoints are preserved. Post-merge evaluation asks about retained specialist breadth, composition, interference, and any recovery training; it is not a generic parent-versus-candidate contest.

## Campaign contract

A campaign records its mode, developmental stage, purpose, success criteria, failure criteria, expected regressions, target capabilities or bootstrap milestones, hypotheses and sought observations, and any declared branches or merge sources. Missing purpose criteria prevent campaign creation.

Training sessions bind the campaign-contract hash, training mode, and declared branch/source into their ordered session plan. Evolutionary training cannot use an undeclared branch. Merge specialist training cannot use an undeclared source.

Evaluation jobs carry an exact context generated from the campaign contract. Mission Hub derives branch completeness from succeeded evaluation jobs bound to the same contract hash; a caller cannot assert that all branches are complete. Diagnostic findings remain visible independently from campaign failure. This separation is what allows a Play-style regression/recovery study to succeed by answering its research question even when a branch does not improve.

## Merge evidence

The Dragon Hatchling paper demonstrates an architecture-specific BDH merge by concatenating tensors along the neuron dimension and averaging other tensors. It also reports post-merge language interference and recovery through joint training. Mission Hub therefore defines merge as a purpose/evidence mode, but does not pretend that a generic merge executor is safe: the exact tensor procedure and compatibility checks must be commissioned for the concrete Ninereeds architecture before execution is enabled.
