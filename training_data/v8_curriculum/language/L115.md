lesson L115
POINT: expressing ownership with plural possessive `s'`
DIFFICULTY: 3
TOPIC: art tools and what they are used for
GROUNDING: IMAGE
VOCAB_SET_1: brush, pencil, pen, marker
VOCAB_SET_2: student, teacher, painter, designer
VOCAB_SET_3: painting, drawing, sketch, poster
PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `Are these the students' brushes? — Yes, these are the students' brushes.`
- NEGATIVE: `Are these the teachers' brushes? — No, these aren't the teachers' brushes; they are the students' brushes.`
- W-QUESTION: `Whose brushes are these? — These are the students' brushes.`
- OR-QUESTION: `Are these the students' brushes or the teachers' brushes? — They are the students' brushes.`
