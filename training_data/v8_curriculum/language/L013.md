lesson L013
POINT: replacing an understood count noun with `one / ones`
DIFFICULTY: 3
TOPIC: things found in a kitchen
GROUNDING: IMAGE
VOCAB_SET_1: cup, plate, bowl, spoon
VOCAB_SET_2: fork, knife, pan, pot
VOCAB_SET_3: kettle, toaster, fridge, oven
PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `Is the striped cup yours? — Yes, the striped one is mine.`
- NEGATIVE: `Is the plain cup yours? — No, the plain one isn't mine.`
- W-QUESTION: `Which cup is yours? — The striped one is mine.`
- OR-QUESTION: `Is your cup the striped one or the plain one? — My cup is the striped one.`
