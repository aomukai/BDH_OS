lesson L144
POINT: expressing a condition with an exception using `unless`
DIFFICULTY: 4
TOPIC: plans that branch when conditions change
GROUNDING: SEQUENCE
VOCAB_SET_1: rain, snow, delay, cancellation
VOCAB_SET_2: continue, stop, stay, leave
VOCAB_SET_3: picnic, trip, match, meeting
PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `Will the picnic continue unless it rains? — Yes, the picnic will continue unless it rains.`
- NEGATIVE: `Will the picnic stop unless it rains? — No, it won't stop unless it rains.`
- W-QUESTION: `What will make the picnic stop? — Rain will make it stop; unless it rains, it will continue.`
- OR-QUESTION: `Will the picnic continue unless it rains, or will it stop even in dry weather? — It will continue unless it rains.`
