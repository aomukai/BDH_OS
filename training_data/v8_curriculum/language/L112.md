POINT: asking and answering about frequency and duration with `how often / how long`
DIFFICULTY: 3
TOPIC: practice routines and training schedules
GROUNDING: SEQUENCE
VOCAB_SET_1: daily, weekly, monthly, yearly
VOCAB_SET_2: once, twice, three times, often
