GROUNDING: IMAGE
VOCAB_SET_1: morning, afternoon, evening, night

PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `Is the concert at eight o'clock on Saturday? — Yes, the concert is at eight o'clock on Saturday.`
- NEGATIVE: `Is the concert in the morning? — No, the concert isn't in the morning; it is in the evening.`
- W-QUESTION: `When is the concert? — It is on Saturday at eight o'clock in the evening.`
- OR-QUESTION: `Is the concert on Friday or on Saturday? — It is on Saturday.`
