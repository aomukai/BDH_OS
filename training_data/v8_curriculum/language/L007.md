POINT: referring to one versus more than one with singular and plural noun phrases and `is / are`
DIFFICULTY: 2
TOPIC: fruits people eat fresh or dried
GROUNDING: IMAGE
VOCAB_SET_1: apple, banana, orange, grape
VOCAB_SET_2: mango, peach, pear, plum
VOCAB_SET_3: raisin, date, fig, apricot
PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `Is this an apple? — Yes, it is an apple.`
- NEGATIVE: `Are those apples? — No, those aren't apples.`
- W-QUESTION: `What are these? — These are apples.`
- OR-QUESTION: `Is this an apple, or are these apples? — These are apples.`
