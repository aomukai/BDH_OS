POINT: attributing a claim or piece of evidence to its source
DIFFICULTY: 4
TOPIC: citations that connect claims to their sources
GROUNDING: TEXT
VOCAB_SET_1: report, article, study, survey
VOCAB_SET_2: agency, mayor, researcher, witness
VOCAB_SET_3: according to, reports, states, claims
PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `Does the health agency report that air quality improved? — Yes. According to the health agency, air quality improved.`
- NEGATIVE: `Did the mayor provide that air-quality figure? — No. The figure comes from the health agency.`
- W-QUESTION: `Who says that air quality improved? — The health agency reports that air quality improved.`
- OR-QUESTION: `Is the claim attributed to the mayor or the health agency? — According to the health agency, air quality improved.`
