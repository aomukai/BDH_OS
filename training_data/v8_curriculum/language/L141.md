POINT: expressing reasons with `because / since / as`
DIFFICULTY: 4
TOPIC: direct causes, contributing causes, and triggers
GROUNDING: TEXT
VOCAB_SET_1: rain, wind, darkness, heat
VOCAB_SET_2: leak, traffic, illness, noise
VOCAB_SET_3: stop, delay, close, fail
PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `Did the match stop because it was raining? — Yes, the match stopped because it was raining.`
- NEGATIVE: `Did the match stop because it was dark? — No, it didn't stop because it was dark.`
- W-QUESTION: `Why did the match stop? — Since it was raining, the match stopped.`
- OR-QUESTION: `Did the match stop because of rain or because of darkness? — As it was raining, the match stopped.`
