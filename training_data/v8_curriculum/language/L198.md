POINT: comparing and synthesizing information from more than one source
DIFFICULTY: 5
TOPIC: repeatable and nonrepeatable results
GROUNDING: TEXT
VOCAB_SET_1: repeatable, consistent, similar, stable
VOCAB_SET_2: nonrepeatable, inconsistent, different, variable
VOCAB_SET_3: study, experiment, result, finding
PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `Do both studies find that trees cool city streets? — Yes. Both studies report cooling, although Study B finds a larger effect.`
- NEGATIVE: `Do the studies reach completely opposite conclusions? — No. Both find cooling, but they disagree about its size.`
- W-QUESTION: `What do the two studies jointly show? — Taken together, they show that trees cool streets, with uncertainty about how much.`
- OR-QUESTION: `Do the sources agree on the direction of the effect or its exact size? — They agree on cooling but differ on its exact size.`
