lesson L111
POINT: asking and answering about quantity with `how much / how many`
DIFFICULTY: 3
TOPIC: countable and measurable quantities
GROUNDING: IMAGE
VOCAB_SET_1: apple, candle, ticket, bottle
VOCAB_SET_2: milk, water, rice, flour
VOCAB_SET_3: gram, kilogram, liter, dozen
PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `How many candles has Mina got? Has she got six? — Yes, Mina has got six candles.`
- NEGATIVE: `How much milk has Mina got? Has she got a liter? — No, she has got only half a liter.`
- W-QUESTION: `How many candles has Mina got? — Mina has got six candles.`
- OR-QUESTION: `How much milk has Mina got, half a liter or one liter? — She has got half a liter.`
