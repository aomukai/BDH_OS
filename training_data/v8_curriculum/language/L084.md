lesson L084
POINT: making a polite request with `would`
DIFFICULTY: 3
TOPIC: doors and windows as controlled openings
GROUNDING: IMAGE
VOCAB_SET_1: door, window, curtain, gate
VOCAB_SET_2: open, close, lock, unlock
VOCAB_SET_3: slide, pull, push, lift
PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `Would you open the curtain, please? — Certainly. I'll open it now.`
- NEGATIVE: `Would you close the curtain, please? — Sorry, I can't close it because the paint is drying.`
- W-QUESTION: `What would you like me to do? — Would you open the curtain, please?`
- OR-QUESTION: `Would you open the curtain or the window, please? — The curtain, please.`
