lesson L042
POINT: forming manner adverbs with `-ly` and placing them after a verb or its object
DIFFICULTY: 2
TOPIC: safe and dangerous situations
GROUNDING: IMAGE
VOCAB_SET_1: carefully, carelessly, safely, dangerously
VOCAB_SET_2: slowly, quickly, quietly, loudly
VOCAB_SET_3: gently, roughly, correctly, incorrectly
PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `Did Jun pack the vase carefully? — Yes, Jun packed the vase carefully.`
- NEGATIVE: `Did Jun pack the vase carelessly? — No, Jun didn't pack the vase carelessly.`
- W-QUESTION: `How did Jun pack the vase? — Jun packed the vase carefully.`
- OR-QUESTION: `Did Jun pack the vase carefully or carelessly? — Jun packed it carefully.`
