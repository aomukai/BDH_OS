lesson L041
POINT: placing frequency adverbs before a lexical verb and after `be` or an auxiliary
DIFFICULTY: 3
TOPIC: frequency and repeated events
GROUNDING: TEXT
VOCAB_SET_1: always, usually, often, sometimes
VOCAB_SET_2: rarely, seldom, hardly ever, never
VOCAB_SET_3: daily, weekly, monthly, yearly
PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `Does Sara always carry a notebook? — Yes, Sara always carries a notebook.`
- NEGATIVE: `Is Sara always late? — No, Sara is never late.`
- W-QUESTION: `What does Sara always carry? — Sara always carries a notebook.`
- OR-QUESTION: `Does Sara always carry a notebook, or does she never carry one? — Sara always carries one.`
