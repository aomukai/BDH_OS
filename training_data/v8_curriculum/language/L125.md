lesson L125
POINT: distinguishing additional and remaining referents with `other / another / the other`
DIFFICULTY: 4
TOPIC: things found in a bedroom
GROUNDING: IMAGE
VOCAB_SET_1: blanket, pillow, sheet, towel
VOCAB_SET_2: bed, chair, lamp, mirror
VOCAB_SET_3: drawer, closet, window, door
PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `Would you like another blanket? — Yes, I want another blanket.`
- NEGATIVE: `Would you like the other blanket? — No, I don't want the other blanket.`
- W-QUESTION: `One blanket is wet. Which one should I use? — Use the other blanket.`
- OR-QUESTION: `Do you need another blanket, or do you want the other one? — I want the other one.`
