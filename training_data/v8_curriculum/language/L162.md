lesson L162
POINT: comparing alternatives and justifying a choice
DIFFICULTY: 5
TOPIC: trade-offs in which gaining one benefit requires giving up another
GROUNDING: TEXT
VOCAB_SET_1: cheaper, cleaner, faster, quieter
VOCAB_SET_2: cost, benefit, advantage, disadvantage
VOCAB_SET_3: bus, car, train, bicycle
PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `Is the electric bus the better option? — Yes. Unlike the diesel bus, it is quiet and produces no exhaust, so I would choose it.`
- NEGATIVE: `Is the diesel bus the better option? — No. It is cheaper, but the electric bus is cleaner and quieter.`
- W-QUESTION: `Which bus would you choose, and why? — I would choose the electric bus because it is cleaner than the diesel one.`
- OR-QUESTION: `Would you choose the electric or diesel bus? — I would choose the electric bus; although it costs more, it pollutes less.`
