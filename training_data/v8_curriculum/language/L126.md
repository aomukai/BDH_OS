POINT: expressing equality and inequality with `(not) as ... as`
DIFFICULTY: 3
TOPIC: size and comparison
GROUNDING: IMAGE
VOCAB_SET_1: long, short, wide, narrow
VOCAB_SET_2: tall, low, thick, thin
VOCAB_SET_3: rope, box, tower, bridge
PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `Is the green rope as long as the blue rope? — Yes, the green rope is as long as the blue rope.`
- NEGATIVE: `Is the red rope as long as the blue rope? — No, the red rope isn't as long as the blue rope.`
- W-QUESTION: `Which rope is as long as the blue one? — The green rope is as long as the blue one.`
- OR-QUESTION: `Is the green rope as long as the blue one, or is it shorter? — It is as long as the blue one.`
