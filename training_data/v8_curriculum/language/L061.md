lesson L061
POINT: expressing predictions, decisions, promises, and offers with `will`
DIFFICULTY: 4
TOPIC: everyday plans, promises, and offers
GROUNDING: SEQUENCE
VOCAB_SET_1: call, visit, meet, travel
VOCAB_SET_2: help, carry, cook, pay
VOCAB_SET_3: tonight, tomorrow, this weekend, Monday
PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `Will you call me tonight? — Yes, I'll call you tonight.`
- NEGATIVE: `Will you leave the bags here? — No, I'll carry them for you.`
- W-QUESTION: `The phone is ringing. What will you do? — I'll answer it.`
- OR-QUESTION: `Will the ferry arrive tonight or tomorrow? — It will arrive tonight.`
