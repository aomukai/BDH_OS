lesson L039
POINT: making an offer with `shall I ...?`
DIFFICULTY: 3
TOPIC: asking for help and offering help
GROUNDING: SEQUENCE
VOCAB_SET_1: carry, lift, hold, move
VOCAB_SET_2: suitcase, backpack, box, bag
VOCAB_SET_3: door, window, taxi, seat
PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `Shall I carry your suitcase? — Yes, please carry my suitcase.`
- NEGATIVE: `Shall I carry your suitcase? — No, thank you; I can manage.`
- W-QUESTION: `What can I do to help? — Shall I carry your suitcase?`
- OR-QUESTION: `Shall I carry your suitcase or your backpack? — Please carry my suitcase.`
