lesson L012
POINT: replacing a possessive noun phrase with `mine / yours / his / hers / ours / theirs`
DIFFICULTY: 3
TOPIC: bicycles and their parts
GROUNDING: IMAGE
VOCAB_SET_1: bicycle, helmet, wheel, seat
VOCAB_SET_2: pedal, chain, bell, basket
VOCAB_SET_3: handlebar, tire, brake, light
PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `Is the red bicycle yours? — Yes, it is mine.`
- NEGATIVE: `Is the red bicycle yours? — No, it isn't mine.`
- W-QUESTION: `Whose is the red bicycle? — It is mine.`
- OR-QUESTION: `Is the red bicycle mine or yours? — It is mine.`
