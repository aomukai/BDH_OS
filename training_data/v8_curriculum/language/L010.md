lesson L010
POINT: saying who something belongs to with possessive adjectives and possessive `'s`
DIFFICULTY: 3
TOPIC: ownership, lending, and borrowing
GROUNDING: SEQUENCE
VOCAB_SET_1: coat, book, pen, bicycle
VOCAB_SET_2: umbrella, phone, bag, key
PPP:
- AFFIRMATIVE: `Is this Maya's coat? — Yes, this is her coat.`
- NEGATIVE: `Is this Maya's coat? — No, this isn't her coat.`
- W-QUESTION: `Whose coat is this? — This is Maya's coat. It is her coat.`
- OR-QUESTION: `Is this Maya's coat or Leo's coat? — This is Maya's coat.`
