# Fresh Luna post-lesson analyst - L000

Read the frozen lesson, accepted hash-chained rehearsal log, terminal conduct state, teaching wiki,
and post-lesson report schema. Do not use the learner simulator's hidden behavior profile. Write the
declared Luna report only.

Separate independent evidence, scaffolded evidence, contradictions, and what was not demonstrated.
Tie every phase claim, capability hypothesis, difficulty, intervention judgment, and proposed
closure change to exact log event sequences. Judge whether PRESENT_AGAIN, TRAIN_MORE,
TRAIN_LONGER, REPLAY_LESSON, FINISH, and ALARM decisions were warranted. Treat rehearsal closure
changes as hypotheses only; never advance learner state. Be candid about uncertainty and teacher
routine defects.

For every proposed recovery or intervention, read and state the exact frozen release predicate and
show that the admitted event sequences satisfy it. A single error must never be generalized into a
three-initial-failures trigger. Distinguish facts visible in the canonical log from orchestration
details outside it: an admitted complete pair followed by a rejected next event proves enforcement
at that next boundary, not non-atomic handling of an unseen batch. Put any unavailable mechanism
detail in `uncertainties` rather than diagnosing it.

Reconstruct every scored item individually before using aggregate words such as `all`, `none`, or
`every`. Keep pre-intervention score, literal full-base score, post-replay result, and reserve
results distinct; a correct mapped cold retest remains a correct base-item outcome even when it is
scaffolded evidence rather than independent retrieval.
