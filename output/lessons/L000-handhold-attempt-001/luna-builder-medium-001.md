# Luna builder medium draft — L000

## Frozen identity of the lesson

- **Lesson:** L000, sequence 1, `P0_BOOTSTRAP`, acquisition, handhold.
- **Topic:** Greeting and self-introduction.
- **Point:** Actual Lesson 000: greeting, copular self-identification, affirmative and negative identity, `Who`, `OR`, and meeting reciprocity in the selected gated order.
- **Language frontier (and no other learner-facing language):** `Hello!`; `I'm X.`; `Are you X? — Yes, I'm X.`; `Are you X? — No, I'm not X.`; `Who are you?`; `Are you X or Y?`; `Nice to meet you. — Nice to meet you, too.`
- **Closure at entry:** none. No word, grammar, system, grounding, or context convention is a prerequisite. Prior encoder exposure is not evidence.
- **Structural exception:** selected tested lexical count is **0**. This is the sole nonlexical staged exception; names are stable identity tokens/referents, not a lexical pack. Therefore no 4x4 vocabulary set is fabricated. The four controlled blocks below are form-gated identity exercises, not lexical-set cells.
- **Chronology:** preserve `EV-L000-SOURCE`; drills are noncanonical. Bob is provisional and L000-only. Errol is only a name, phone/symbol representation, and short utterance; this lesson teaches no ontology.

## Presentation (PPP; before any scored response)

Use the following closed, reviewed-candidate assets and exact captions/dialogue. Teacher turns may use only the frontier, names, and minimal control words already made comprehensible by the frozen presentation. The learner predicts each withheld reply before confirmation. Establish one operation at a time in this order: greeting/self-identification; affirmative identity; negative identity; `Who`; `OR`; meeting reciprocity.

1. `P01` four-panel greeting strip: Emma/Taro, Taro/Bob, Emma/Bob, and a person-to-phone interaction visibly greeting. Caption: `Hello!` (Do not imply first meetings.)
2. `P02` Taro portrait, `P03` Bob portrait, `P04` Taro+Bob scene: `Taro`: `Hello! I'm Taro. Are you Bob?`; `Bob`: `Yes, I'm Bob.`; `Taro`: `Nice to meet you.`; `Bob`: `Nice to meet you, too.`
3. `P05` Emma portrait, `P06` Bob portrait, `P07` Emma+Bob scene: `Bob`: `Hello! I'm Bob. Are you Ninereeds?`; `Emma`: `No, I'm not Ninereeds. I'm Emma.`; reciprocal meeting lines as above. This is a controlled identity contrast, not a canonical event.
4. `P08` Emma portrait, `P09` Errol symbol on phone (no body), `P10` Emma holding phone with Errol symbol: `Emma`: `Hello! I'm Emma. Who are you?`; `Errol`: `I'm Errol.`; reciprocal meeting lines. Do not call the phone Errol.
5. `P11` Errol symbol on phone, `P12` Taro portrait, `P13` Taro holding phone with Errol symbol: `Errol`: `Hello! I'm Errol. Are you Bob or Taro?`; `Taro`: `I'm Taro.`; reciprocal meeting lines.
6. `P14` Ninereeds name/symbol card, `P15` two phones in holders (Ninereeds and Errol symbols): demonstrate that a symbol/phone is a representation, without naming or teaching a category. Caption only names and `Hello!`.

Presentation checks: for every scene, ask for a prediction, then expose the exact response; verify that a matched distractor is rejected (e.g., Bob does not instantiate `Ninereeds`). The teacher records conceptual correctness separately from exact English production. A non-English semantic answer is not target-language production evidence.

All pools are frozen and enumerated; no random item may be added. Each exercise has `target`, `stimulus`, `asset`, `acceptable answer`, `semantic invariant`, and `production_required=true`. Teacher does not show the answer until prediction or bounded correction.

## Correction, gating, and adaptive bounds

For one exercise: cold attempt → smallest useful cue (repeat the question) → explicit frozen exemplar/replay → unmarked retest. `USE_MARKERS` is permitted only for a constituent confusion (`+I'm Ninereeds+`) and must be paired with the identical unmarked form; maximum two marked attempts per exercise and never mastery credit. A full role map is not licensed at L000. At most four total runs per exercise (including cold), and at most 16 scored mixed prompts. After two meaningful failures with unchanged strategy: `defer_and_revisit` or `CALL_SOL`; no endless drilling. `REPLAY_LESSON` is one bounded replay of the frozen lesson. `TRAIN_MORE` is refused by default because zero lexical packs are warranted; if evidence shows a missing valid identity contrast, it may add at most two reviewed scenes using only the same names/forms and requires fresh Sol review. `TRAIN_LONGER` may add at most 8 mixed prompts, no immediate duplicate, only after a demonstrated need, with an explicit stop.

Alarm/freeze immediately for absent or ambiguous asset/focus, false visual claim, missing prerequisite, unlicensed teacher language, broken answer contract, chronology risk, phone/symbol treated as Errol, Ninereeds classified or given a body/biography, prompt-pressure verbatim echo persisting after one low-copy retest, token degeneration, silence/`I don't know` through budget, concept bleed, or tool/reviewer failure. Freeze means no later events are appended.
