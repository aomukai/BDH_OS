## Language boundary and PPP execution contract

### Learner-facing surface

The only learner-facing forms are the exact source forms below, the five names, and their permitted
substitutions in the closed exercise tables. A UI heading such as `Today we learn: Greeting and
self-introduction` is system metadata, not a new learner-language item. Operational events (`SHOW`,
`ASK`, `EXPECT`, `ACCEPT`, `CORRECT`, `RETEST`) are machine controls and are not spoken. No teacher
turn may add “look,” “repeat,” “try again,” “this is,” a new pronoun, an explanation, or a biography.

Every turn records four language ledgers: `known=[]` (there is no established language at entry),
`frontier` (the exact form being exposed), `instruction` (machine event), and `rescue` (one matching
source replay). Semantic/conceptual correctness and target-language production are independent:
an answer in another language can be concept-correct but has `target_language_production=false`.
Teacher-prompt copying is anti-evidence; the controller must switch to a non-copyable visual
elicitation over the same frozen asset and require an unmarked retest.

### PPP map (same gates, explicit roles)

1. **Presentation:** P1–P4 demonstrate the source lesson in exact order, with reviewed visual
   claims and no scored learner turn.
2. **Practice:** affirmative/yes, negative/no, Who, and OR are four separate controlled gates,
   each with four closed exercises. This is the source's original order.
3. **Production/mixed:** Kreuz und Quer interleaves only forms already presented and controlled.
4. **Picture book and comprehension:** the current operator policy requires these after mixed
   practice; they are a noncanonical instructional restaging and closed comprehension set.
5. **Recap:** five unmarked retrievals close the lesson; they do not update learner state.

No gate is added or removed. Picture book and comprehension are policy-required dual-use additions
to the preserved source lesson, not a rewrite of the source's original controlled-gate order.

## Bounded intervention, correction, and gating

The controller records cold attempt, meaning, target-language production, failure class, visual
focus, intervention, support level, and lower-support retest. It follows the smallest useful
intervention:

1. Echo only the suspicious fragment or re-present the same visual focus.
2. If an item fails, replay its matching P1–P4 source exemplar once, with no new language, then
   retest the identical item unmarked.
3. For a single role/order confusion, use one constituent-only marker, for example
   `+[Are you Ninereeds?]+`; immediately show the identical unmarked sentence and require unmarked
   output. A broad role-map overlay (two bounded visual role bands, no added learner text) is
   permitted only when the trace shows broad role confusion; it is paired with the identical
   unmarked sentence and an unmarked retest.
4. For a new grammar-frontier focus, the marked span may use `+...+` around only that frontier;
   never improvise delimiter meanings. Marked output is teaching evidence, never mastery evidence.
5. For affirmative/negative confusion, replay the P1/P2 contrast and retest; do not add names,
   clauses, facts, or operations.
6. After the first unmarked recovery on an item, fade support. A copied marker receives a bounded
   check and an unmarked retest; it is not a hit.
7. One item has at most four total runs including cold attempt. An unchanged strategy may not be
   repeated after two meaningful failure episodes; exhaustion yields `defer_and_revisit` or
   `problem_item`.

Global bounds: maximum 120 learner/teacher turns; one matching presentation replay per item;
marker budget 8; at most one `REPLAY_LESSON`; mixed base cap 16 scored rounds. Permitted controller
actions are `CONTINUE`, `PRESENT_AGAIN`, `USE_MARKERS`, `TRAIN_MORE`, `TRAIN_LONGER`, `BACKTRACK`,
`CALL_SOL`, and `FINISH`. `BACKTRACK` returns only to the immediately preceding gate. `CALL_SOL`
freezes the exact turn, transcript, assets, state, interventions, remaining budgets, and reason;
no later events are appended until a bounded disposition exists.
