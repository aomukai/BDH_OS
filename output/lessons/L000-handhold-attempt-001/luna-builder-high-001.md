## Bounded correction and adaptive controls

1. Cold attempt: show frozen full pair and prompt; record response, conceptual correctness, target
language production, and error class.
2. On a single-item failure, replay its matching P1–P4 exemplar once with no new language, then
retest the identical item unmarked.
3. For role/order confusion, one constituent marker may be used, for example
+[Are you Ninereeds?]+. Pair the marked sentence with its identical unmarked form and require
unmarked output. Marked output never counts as mastery.
4. For negative/affirmative confusion, use the matched P1/P2 contrast and retest unmarked; do not
add a name, clause, or world claim.
5. Each failure episode has at most four total runs including cold. Do not repeat an unchanged
strategy after two meaningful failure episodes; exhaustion yields defer_and_revisit/problem_item.
6. BACKTRACK can return only to the immediately preceding gate. CALL_SOL freezes the exact turn
when interpretation or truth is unsafe. FINISH is allowed only at a bounded stop.

Bounds: one replay per exemplar per item; maximum 120 learner/teacher turns; base mixed cap 16
scored rounds; at most one full REPLAY_LESSON. Marker budget is 8 globally, fading after the
first unmarked success on an item. Marker recovery always gets an unmarked immediate retest.
Permitted actions are CONTINUE, PRESENT_AGAIN, USE_MARKERS, TRAIN_MORE, TRAIN_LONGER, BACKTRACK,
CALL_SOL, FINISH.
