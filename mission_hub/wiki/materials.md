# Materials

## Foundational English curriculum v6

The current prepared curriculum is the Sol v6 bundle under `docs/curriculum_v6_sol/`.
Its machine-readable acquisition sequence contains 396 acquisition lessons. The
separate delayed-rehearsal layer contains 270 later integration entries. Both are
prepared designs, not authorization to train, and remain subject to independent audit,
lesson compilation, visual readiness, Instructor qualification, and learner-state
gates.

Earlier v1–v5 outputs are provenance for the synthesis, not competing current
curricula. They are archived after their useful design choices and disagreements were
carried into v6 and its synthesis-decision record.

## Near-term diagnostic checkpoints

The prepared cadence inserts one diagnostic checkpoint after each eight completed
acquisition lessons and one after a final partial block. Applied mechanically to 396
acquisitions, this is 50 checkpoints, producing 716 planned conducted lessons when
combined with 270 delayed rehearsals, before conditional remediation. This arithmetic
describes the current plan; it is not a demonstrated optimal cadence.

The historical Lesson 000 draft and supplied human-course repetition PDF remain
registered archived references. They supplied examples and constraints but are not
executable authority. In particular, world chronology, first meetings, prerequisites,
and exact exercise structure come from the current curriculum and lesson contracts.

## Frozen-corpus visual hygiene audit before training

The operator-authorized foundation set receives an independent DeepSeek opinion on every final
vocabulary target together with its complete ten-image pack. Freeze the corrected lexicon and
asset manifest first; an audit of a moving corpus is not evidence about the eventual training
input. DeepSeek's PASS, FAIL, and UNCERTAIN verdicts are hygiene evidence only. They do not veto
the operator's authorization, mutate the set, create remediation automatically, or block
training. Protocol completion requires exact coverage and binding to the proposed input hash.

Before pixel review, every target must also pass the no-undefined-words gate: it needs a
non-empty canonical lexical label, one positive locked definition of what it means, and a valid
part of speech or explicitly defined lexical class. Undefined tokens, placeholders, metadata
labels, the catch-all class `other`, and the form-only class `phrase` are not teachable
vocabulary. A multiword lexical unit is allowed only when the whole unit has one defined sense
and an actual grammatical role such as noun, adverb, quantifier, or interjection.

The request unit is one locked teaching sense plus all ten numbered images. The reviewer checks
individual sense fit and defects as well as collective variation and shortcut risk. Preserve the
exact request, response, model settings, token usage, asset hashes, and input-manifest hash.
DeepSeek findings are independent evidence, not authority to delete or replace assets. `FAIL`
and `UNCERTAIN` remain preserved for optional later inspection, with no automatic Luna, Sol,
generation, or replacement route. The audit protocol is satisfied when every pack has a preserved
opinion and the audited manifest hash exactly matches the proposed training input.
The executable contract is
`mission_hub/research/policies/campaign36-deepseek-vl-pretraining-audit.json`.
