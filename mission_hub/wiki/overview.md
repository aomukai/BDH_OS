# Research overview

## Current stage

Campaign 35 and its authorized post-campaign reconstruction are complete. The
pipeline is paused, M3/M4/M5 and reconstructed sessions 20/25 are preserved, no
foundational base has been selected, and no successor campaign is authorized. The
next action is deliberate Sol planning, not automatic continuation.

## Established within Campaign 35

- Image-only training produced an executable visual substrate distinct from the
  language sibling, though terminal held-out retrieval was not demonstrated.
- The M1/M2 neuron-axis merge was mechanically valid: its audit passed all declared
  tensor checks. Mechanical correctness did not preserve M3's learned joint
  coordination or measured behavior.
- Exact M3 replay moved M4 through transient low-pathology states, demonstrating
  partial functional repair, but healing was unstable and non-monotonic. No
  language-capable terminal passed a behavioral case.
- Original and reconstructed session 20 checkpoints had nearly identical macro
  representation geometry and the same response uniqueness, but different
  neuron-level organization and different responses to sessions 21–25.
- Near-identical loss trajectories did not imply equivalent gate response, optimizer
  movement, relative parameter updates, representation retention, or later behavior.

The resulting durable principle is: behavioral equivalence does not imply
representational equivalence, and coarse representational similarity does not imply
identical future learning dynamics. Controlled continuation response is part of a
checkpoint's experimentally relevant phenotype.

## Evidence limits

- “Healed” currently means reduced pathological generation, not broad competence.
- The original session 20 bytes were deleted; tensor-only identity is unavailable.
- Concept-neuron overlap is a diagnostic summary, not complete coordinate proof.
- One reconstruction shows possible divergence but not its distribution across seeds.
- M3/M4 and M5 terminal modality probes were not like-for-like, and retrieval remained
  zero in the tested terminal cross-modal probe.

See [Campaign 0035 findings](campaigns/campaign_0035_findings.md) for artifacts,
numbers, anomalies, and question-level evidence boundaries.

## Planning frontier

Every checkpoint intended for later teaching, merging, healing, or grafting should be
preserved and receive a frozen short continuation challenge. Selection must report
current capability, stability, plasticity, interference resistance, representation
retention, optimizer movement, and reacquisition cost without hiding the tradeoffs in
one score.

Two successor directions are prepared for comparison, not authorization:

1. a bounded latent-iteration feasibility study on preserved compatible checkpoints;
2. four separate 150M English, German, Japanese, and Chinese visual-language births,
   followed by human-style monolingual schooling and later controlled modular merging.

They should not be combined by default. The first isolates an architectural compute
path; the second changes scale, language, curriculum, and eventual integration. Sol
must decide which uncertainty is more valuable, identify prerequisite work, define
replication and continuation probes, and only then propose a successor goal.

The maintained [teaching methodology](teaching.md) records the multilingual visual-
birth, Luna-conducted dialogue, Ask me role reversal, prepared visual focus, adaptive
word-pack, and developmental identity candidates. The [research questions](questions.md)
make their unresolved design and evidence requirements explicit.
