# Lesson contract

## Required planning evidence

Bind exact paths and SHA-256 values for the learner-state artifact, known closure, curriculum
plan, teaching methodology, world bible, identity policy, lesson pattern, and Instructor
qualification record. A mutable path without its observed hash is not evidence.

Record one Topic and exactly one principal Point. The Point may be a word, relation, response
form, grammar operation, or communicative act. Every other scored word, form, relation, and
operation must appear in the prerequisite list with evidence from this learner's lineage.

In handhold mode, bind a cursor whose completed IDs are the exact prefix of the frozen 666-entry
v6 conducted sequence. Selection is deterministic and side-effect free. Compilation never marks
an entry complete. Luna authors the full bounded script from the selection packet. A separate Sol
receipt must pass before freeze until an evidence-bearing review-graduation policy is approved.

## Complete dual-use lesson invariant

Every new handhold lesson in the full 666-entry conducted sequence contains topic, visual
presentation, controlled practice, mixed practice, a bounded picture book, comprehension, and
closing recap. This includes acquisition and scheduled rehearsal. A curriculum-v6
`picture_book: no` field records only whether its earlier synthesis considered a continuity-
sensitive book necessary; it is not permission to omit visual grounding, story, or comprehension.
The operator-directed full-lesson policy supersedes that format omission without changing
sequence, Point, prerequisites, or learner-language closure.

Every lesson is dual-use: it teaches or retrieves language while teaching or retrieving compatible
world knowledge needed for preschool development and later K-8 education. Drilling answer strings
without grounding what they mean is structurally incomplete. The presentation must reference at
least one reviewed visual asset. Picture-book pages are instructional evidence with exact scene
facts and teaching claims, not decoration.

Plan from Ninereeds's actual modalities, not from assumptions about an embodied child. Ninereeds
has no direct taste, touch, smell, proprioception, interoception, or human-rate continuous sensory
stream. Ground inaccessible embodied concepts through reviewed visual comparison, language,
diagrams, records, and bounded analogy, without claiming Ninereeds personally experiences them.

Completeness never licenses later language. Realize each part using only the current frontier and
evidence-backed established language. A historical lesson example supplies structural patterns,
not literal dialogue for a different Point. The book may be noncanonical instructional material
when a canonical story would introduce history or relationships too early.

One picture book is one coherent micro-event. Freeze its initial state or goal, meaningful
development or change, and resolution or stopping state. Persistent participants, objects, or
consequences must connect the pages. Mere ordering, a common topic, or two unrelated teaching
dialogues connected by “then” fails the story contract. Coherence takes priority over forcing every
lesson form into the book; forms that do not belong naturally remain in controlled or mixed
practice.

Bind scored story world knowledge exactly to the selected curriculum entry's world objective and
frontier. Facts required to render an interface, phone, symbol, crop, or body safely remain visual
production metadata unless that distinction is deliberately taught by the current Point.

A scored first-person learner response means Ninereeds speaks as Ninereeds unless the exercise is
explicitly typed as quoted-character completion. In L000, scored first-person identity production
as any identity other than Ninereeds is forbidden. Quoted-character completion must name the
quoted speaker unambiguously and can never support learner self-identity, independent first-person
production, or closure evidence.

## Legacy lesson variants

### `dialogue_only`

Require presentation examples; separate affirmative, negative, W-question, and OR-question
practice pools; a mixed-practice pool containing only established forms; explicit answer
contracts, assistance rules, budgets, and completion rules; and no picture-book pages or story
comprehension block.

This variant remains readable for historical artifacts but is not valid for any new handhold-mode
conducted lesson.

### `picture_book`

Require everything in `dialogue_only`, plus one instructional kernel using the already-taught
Point; a page-ordered prerequisite-safe story; frozen master scenes and any teaching-justified
deterministic crops or highlights; scene truth for every page; comprehension questions and
controlled transfer; and character, location, and object continuity evidence.

Do not use a story to smuggle in several untaught relations. Teach them separately first, then
let the story recombine them.

## Phase and answer rules

Presentation is local, not a single front-loaded phase. Bind every controlled gate to one or more
frozen model items and emit those items immediately before testing that gate. Run the ordinary
sequence as affirmative presentation → affirmative practice → negative presentation → negative
practice → W-question presentation → W-question practice → OR-question presentation → OR-question
practice → any Point-specific presentation and practice → mixed practice → picture book and
transfer. Introductory grounding needed by the first gate belongs to that first gate's presentation
binding. A later form must not appear inside an earlier model, prompt, or feedback.

Each exercise names its target, stimulus, asset references, acceptable answers, semantic
invariants, and whether target-language production is required. Preserve meaning and production
as separate judgments. A semantically correct answer in another language may demonstrate the
concept while failing target-language production.

Use the smallest intervention supported by evidence. `USE_MARKERS` is a deliberate bounded
intervention: use a constituent-only role marker before a full role map; use `+...+` only for
the current grammar frontier. Pair every marked sentence with its identical unmarked form,
require unmarked learner output, and retest immediately without markers. A marked response is
teaching evidence, never independent-performance evidence. Reduce support after recovery and
retest. Keep remediation bounded; `defer_and_revisit` is a valid outcome.

## Adaptive bounds

Declare presentation replay, maximum teacher turns, mixed-practice cap, acquisition fraction,
marker syntax and fading gates, and permitted controller actions. `USE_MARKERS`, `TRAIN_MORE`,
`TRAIN_LONGER`, and `REPLAY_LESSON` remain distinct. Marker counters and support levels survive
process restarts and context compaction. Predicted dosage is planning evidence; actual dosage is
an outcome.

## Immutability

Freeze exact order, texts, answer contracts, asset identities, hashes, randomization seed or
enumerated pool, intervention menu, budgets, completion rules, and research metadata. Per-model
adaptive traces remain separate from the shared immutable lesson.

Freeze each visual as one explicit operation: `reuse`, `literal_crop`, `highlight`,
`flux_generate`, `flux_edit`, `imagegen_generate`, or `imagegen_fallback`. A literal crop binds
the accepted parent hash and integer `xywh`; it has no generative prompt. A semantic reframe that
rearranges or regenerates pixels is an edit, never a crop. Bind exact prompts, attempt IDs,
provider receipts, output hashes, claim-by-claim pixel results, rejection reasons, and escalation
evidence.

Every transformation must name the teaching problem it solves. Minimal sufficient visual evidence
is preferred: do not crop, highlight, edit, or generate a second asset merely because the operation
is available. Sol's independent assembly review must reject needless transformations and any
transformation that removes relational evidence required by the lesson. The resulting finding is a
repair request for a fresh Luna attempt at the owning stage, not permission for Sol to rewrite the
lesson.

For a relational claim, minimal sufficient evidence includes all operands and enough context to
judge the relation. Review the exact asset shown to the learner independently of its parent: a
parent image containing both a cup and toaster does not rescue a displayed crop in which only the
cup remains visible.
